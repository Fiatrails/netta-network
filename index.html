The Problem

Multilateral netting reaches 18 currencies. Everything else nets bilaterally, or not at all.

Multilateral net settlement is the most efficient arrangement in payments, and it is confined to one perimeter. CLS settles 18 currencies, none added since 2015, for 76 settlement members and some 35,000 third parties, around US$7 trillion a day, and reduces funding requirements by around 96% inside that perimeter on its reported basis. Beyond those 18 currencies, what exists is bilateral. CLS’s own netting-calculation service spans more than 120 currencies for 121 committed banks and reports a 43% funding reduction in its published case study — against around 96% for multilateral settlement inside the core. That gap is architectural, not operational: bilateral netting can only offset what two parties owe each other. Beyond both, the Bank for International Settlements estimates that US$1.25 trillion a day of foreign exchange settles with no payment-versus-payment protection at all.

Gross settlement means pre-positioned liquidity. Nostro buffers run two to three times daily need in major currencies and five to ten times in volatile emerging-market currencies, on industry estimates, and every transaction carries T+2 to T+5 of exposure. The result is trapped capital, warehoused FX risk and structurally weak return on equity, even where volumes are strong.

How it works

Compress obligations. Settle only the residual.

Institutions record obligations, NETTA computes the multilateral net, and each participant's own settlement bank moves only what remains. NETTA holds no funds, executes no FX and is never a counterparty.

  1. Record. Obligations are submitted by API or ISO 20022 while the corridor's netting window is open.
  2. Reference-price. Each obligation is valued at a reference rate fixed when it is recorded; the protocol never executes FX.
  3. Net. At window close the multilateral net position of every participant is computed on the ledger; positions offset across corridors only where they share a settlement currency.
  4. Settle. Each net position is executed by the participant's own licensed settlement institution over its existing rails, backed just-in-time by its counter-attestation. Technical execution is atomic; legal finality is subject to per-corridor legal opinions.

What changes for Treasury

Net exposure, your own settlement bank, existing rails

Outbound, your cash stays in normal operational use until the settlement instant; pre-positioned liquidity is eliminated where the rail permits just-in-time funding and materially reduced where it does not. Inbound, the credit you extend against payouts is shortened to the net residual. That residual settles against your own settlement bank's counter-attestation, on rails you already use. Obligations are valued at reference rates fixed at window open; the protocol executes no FX, so you hedge the net, not the gross. Instructions and statements arrive as ISO 20022 CBPR+ messages, validated end-to-end against the official schemas; Swift conformance testing is a pre-pilot step and no certification is claimed. Nothing is held by NETTA at any point.

Illustrative: a US$100M-a-month corridor under legacy arrangements typically requires US$15–25M of pre-funded nostro, T+2 to T+3 settlement and 3–5% all-in cost; netted, the same corridor requires settlement capital of US$5–10M, same-day confirmation and automated reconciliation.

Services

One settlement network, five settlement domains

Settlement

Multilateral netting of institutional cross-border obligations. Reference rates locked at window open; counter-attested just-in-time settlement; ISO 20022 CBPR+ instructions; fiat-only mode on conventional rails.

Settlement model →

PayNet

A licensed aggregation partner — a bank, mobile-money operator or payment institution — converts high-volume, small-value customer flows into netting-eligible institutional obligations under its own licence and compliance programme.

RiskNet

Risk before settlement. Participant-configured exposure and concentration limits are enforced before an obligation enters a netting window; credit decisions remain with each participant.

AssetNet

Delivery-versus-payment netting for tokenised securities, with issuer risk disclosed per asset and settlement gated on finality of both legs.

StableNet

Netting settled in registered tokenised deposits or regulated stablecoins, tier-classified with issuer risk disclosed; never described in central-bank-money terms.

Documentation for each domain is available to qualified institutions under NDA. Further domains — trade finance, energy and autonomous-agent clearing — are in design.

Perimeter and standards

What NETTA is, and the standards it holds itself to

NETTA IS

  • A settlement coordination technology platform
  • A corridor-level multilateral netting engine
  • A non-custodial infrastructure layer
  • A technology service provider, not a designated FMI; no designation is claimed
  • ISO 20022 CBPR+ native

NETTA IS NOT

  • A bank or deposit-taking institution
  • A payment service provider
  • A custodian of funds
  • A market maker or liquidity provider
  • An FX dealer or principal

Privacy by Qedis

Verifiable confidentiality with no single-party key, no trusted hardware and no trusted setup; post-quantum by design. Privacy by Qedis, licensed to the NETTA group.

Post-quantum cryptography

ML-DSA (FIPS 204) signatures and ML-KEM (FIPS 203) key encapsulation, hybrid with ECDSA for migration.

Risk before settlement

Participant-configured limits are enforced before an obligation enters a window; credit decisions remain with each participant.

ISO 20022 CBPR+

Native pacs.008 and pacs.009; pain.001, pacs.002, camt.053 and camt.054 via the institutional API. Validated against the official schemas and CBPR+ usage guidelines; Swift conformance is a pre-pilot step and no certification is claimed. Settlement instructions are generated natively and deterministically at the settlement layer. Machine-assisted translation is used only to normalise inbound payment-provider message formats into canonical obligations, with amounts and identifiers tokenised before processing and results cached deterministically; an untranslatable message is held and flagged, never settled.

PFMI

Designed and operated to the standard expected of a financial market infrastructure though not one: self-assessed against all 24 CPMI-IOSCO Principles, being extended to Annex F for critical service providers; available to participants and their supervisors on request.

Sanctions and Travel Rule

NETTA performs no customer due diligence and holds no customer relationship; KYC, AML and Travel Rule obligations sit with the licensed participants. Obligations are screened at admission against published sanctions lists and rejected before they enter a netting window; every screening outcome is recorded for participants and their supervisors.
