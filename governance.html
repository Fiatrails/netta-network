How the Foundation is governed

Reserved matters, written notice, open registers

A Board Governance Protocol and Delegated Authority Schedule, adopted in August 2026, fixes what the Board decides before anything is executed and how the executive reports on everything else.

Reserved to the Board before execution

  • Every matter in which the Founder or any director has an interest, regardless of value
  • All intellectual-property assignments, licences and security interests
  • Every equity grant or issuance by any group entity
  • Any token-related instrument — the Foundation's token objects are dormant and no token exists or is planned
  • Regulatory submissions in the Foundation's name
  • Senior appointments, borrowing and guarantees, structural transactions, the annual budget

How it runs

  • Reserved matters are put to the Board by written Decision Notice with at least three business days' notice; silence is never treated as consent
  • A Register of Instruments, a Decision Register and a Capitalisation Register, visible to every director; every executed instrument entered within five business days
  • A written Board Note to all directors each week during active periods
  • A convened Board meeting at least quarterly and an annual general meeting each calendar year
  • Directors' interests declared and recorded; interested directors do not decide the matters in which they are interested

The Foundation is a Cayman Islands exempted foundation company with no members and no shareholders. Its constitution prohibits any distribution of income or property to directors or supervisors other than authorised remuneration for services, and it cannot enter enterprise, customer or revenue-generating contracts. Commercial activity sits in FiatRails Holdings Ltd and its operating subsidiaries.
