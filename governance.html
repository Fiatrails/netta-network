Board of Directors

The Founder and three independent non-executive directors

FiatRails Foundation Ltd is governed by a board of four: the Founder and three independent non-executive directors, each with equal standing and no executive chair. Every director and the executive makes an annual conflicts declaration and declares transaction-specific interests; interested directors do not decide the matters in which they are interested. The Board acts as a whole; committees will be constituted as the group scales.

The composition of the Board is published on appointment.

Executive

Vincent Scaturchio, Founder and Chief Executive, is the group’s executive and a director of each entity. Engineering is delivered by Digitalyze Labs Ltd under an arrangement disclosed to the Board. The operating team is funded by the seed round; senior appointments above the Protocol’s thresholds are reserved to the Board.

Risk and compliance oversight

NETTA is pre-pilot. Risk, security and compliance oversight sits with the Board under the Governance Protocol, with a designated security lead and an escalation path to the executive and the Board; independent second-line and internal-audit functions are established as part of the pilot programme.

How the Foundation is governed

Reserved matters, written notice, open registers

A Board Governance Protocol and Delegated Authority Schedule fixes what the Board decides before anything is executed and how the executive reports on everything else.

Reserved to the Board before execution

  • Every matter in which the Founder or any director has an interest, regardless of value
  • All intellectual-property assignments, licences and security interests
  • Every equity grant or issuance by any group entity
  • Any token-related instrument — no protocol token exists or is planned
  • Regulatory submissions in the Foundation’s name
  • Senior appointments, borrowing and guarantees, structural transactions, the annual budget

How it runs

  • Reserved matters are put to the Board by written Decision Notice with notice; silence is never treated as consent
  • Registers of executed instruments and Board decisions, open to every director
  • Regular written reporting to all directors
  • A convened Board meeting at least quarterly and an annual general meeting each calendar year, as its Articles require
  • Directors’ interests declared and recorded; interested directors do not decide the matters in which they are interested

The Foundation is a Cayman Islands exempted foundation company with no members and no shareholders. Its constitution prohibits distributions to directors or supervisors, and it cannot enter enterprise, customer or revenue-generating contracts. Commercial activity sits in FiatRails Holdings Ltd and its operating subsidiaries; briefing requests are passed to FiatRails Settlement Ltd, the contracting entity.

Standards and supervisory engagement

Built to be examined

NETTA is a technology service provider, not a designated financial market infrastructure, and no designation is claimed. We nonetheless design and operate to the standard expected of one.

PFMI

Self-assessed against all 24 principles

Assessed against the CPMI-IOSCO Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures using the CPMI-IOSCO Disclosure Framework, with the principles that cannot apply to a non-custodial provider identified as such, and maintained on a scheduled review cycle. Because participating institutions will treat NETTA as a critical service provider under their own regimes, the assessment is being extended to the CPMI-IOSCO Annex F oversight expectations and to the outsourcing and operational-resilience rules those institutions apply, including the UAE, Singapore, UK and EU frameworks. Available to participating institutions and their supervisors on request.

ISO 20022

CBPR+ native

Settlement instructions and position reporting are generated natively as ISO 20022 CBPR+ messages, validated against the official schemas and the CBPR+ usage guidelines in automated testing. Swift conformance testing is a pre-pilot step and no Swift certification is claimed.

Perimeter

Regulatory dialogue

NETTA has held documented dialogue with financial regulators and central-bank innovation functions in the UAE, Singapore, the United Kingdom and the European Union, and holds a home-jurisdiction legal opinion on the regulatory perimeter of its activity. That dialogue is informal and exploratory. No regulator has licensed, approved or endorsed NETTA or any FiatRails entity, and no sandbox admission or system designation is claimed. NETTA never holds funds, never executes FX and never acts as counterparty; all licensable activity remains with participating institutions.

Technical execution is atomic; legal finality is a matter for per-corridor legal opinions, commissioned as each pilot jurisdiction is confirmed.

Operational resilience and assurance

Designed to pause, never to be wrong

Resilience

NETTA runs on a permissioned settlement network whose validators are known institutional entities; no anonymous validator exists. The system is designed to pause rather than produce an incorrect result: the worst outcome of any infrastructure failure is delayed settlement, never wrong settlement, and settled positions and attestations are preserved in every failure mode. The business-continuity framework is designed to ISO 22301 and specifies monthly failover testing and quarterly recovery drills as the operating cadence to be met from the pilot programme onward. Test-network halts have been recovered without forks or state loss, each documented in a post-mortem available to participants and their supervisors on request, and followed by a hardened upgrade policy. Participants and, where relevant, corridor regulators are notified of material incidents, with a post-incident report to all affected parties.

Control and assurance

No single party holds a decryption key. Supervisory visibility is reconstructed only under threshold, multi-party control with hardware-secured key shares held by separate entities in separate jurisdictions, and every reconstruction and delegated access is recorded on-chain. Protocol changes pass through tiered, time-locked multi-signature authority visible on-chain before execution, with rollback and incident reporting.

What is not yet done. The system has been exercised on testnet and verified on-chain through internal adversarial review; an external security and cryptography audit, ISO 27001 certification and SOC 2 reporting are funded, outstanding milestones. No external certification is claimed.
