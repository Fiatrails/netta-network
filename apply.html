Institutional Briefing

Request an institutional briefing

A briefing is a 45-minute session with the Founder and Chief Executive covering the settlement model, the perimeter analysis for your jurisdiction and the pilot structure. We acknowledge every request within two business days; technical documentation follows an NDA.

What happens next
  1. Acknowledgement within two business days from briefings@netta.network.
  2. Briefing — a 45-minute session with the Founder and Chief Executive.
  3. NDA and documentation pack — the settlement model and corridor rulebook outline, the testnet corridor list, the regulatory record and the PFMI self-assessment.

Prefer email? Write to briefings@netta.network. Pilots and participation agreements are contracted with FiatRails Settlement Ltd, Abu Dhabi Global Market.
